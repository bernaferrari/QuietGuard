# QuietGuard privacy policy

Effective October 31, 2026. Developer: Bernardo Ferrari. Contact: bernaferrari2@gmail.com.

QuietGuard is an on-device firewall. When you enable it, Android's VPN service directs network packets to the app so it can apply your rules. The normal firewall does not send your traffic to a QuietGuard server. You may optionally configure a SOCKS5 proxy; allowed traffic sent through it is handled by that proxy's operator.

To let you configure per-app rules, QuietGuard reads the list of installed apps and their identifiers. It stores settings, firewall rules, and connection records locally. These records can include app identities, addresses, domains, ports, and whether connections were allowed or blocked. Optional packet captures may contain network data. The app does not send these records or the installed-app list to the developer for analytics or advertising. The app has no account or developer analytics service.

You control optional exports, packet captures, custom hosts-file downloads, and any proxy you configure. Data you choose to export or share is handled by the receiving app or service. A custom hosts URL contacts its server when you request the download. The Play build does not use the bundled update-check or default hosts-download endpoints. Other apps and websites you access through your connection have their own privacy practices.

Rules, logs, and preferences are kept on your device. You can clear app data or uninstall QuietGuard to remove its app-private data; files you exported separately remain where you saved them. Android backup for the app is disabled. Device security and any copies you create affect the privacy of locally stored data.

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The current version is published at https://github.com/bernaferrari/QuietGuard/blob/main/PRIVACY.md.
