<p><b>Decide which apps reach the internet.</b> QuietGuard is a private firewall for Android that needs no root. Give each app its own Wi-Fi and mobile data switch, see who tried to connect, and keep all of it on your phone.</p><p><b>Two switches per app</b></p><ul><li>Allow or block Wi-Fi and mobile data separately, with one tap in the app list</li><li>Let video wait for Wi-Fi while the camera, the keyboard or a game never connects at all</li><li>Search the list, filter it to blocked or allowed apps, and open any app for its full rules</li></ul><p><b>A log you can read</b></p><ul><li>Every connection shows the app, the domain or address, and whether it got through</li><li>Filter to blocked connections and group them by app to spot what phones home</li><li>Get a notification when a newly installed app wants the internet</li></ul><p><b>Rules for the edge cases</b></p><ul><li>Allow an app only while the screen is on</li><li>Block apps while roaming, or lock everything down except the apps you trust</li><li>Choose what new apps get by default, and how system apps, tethering and your local network are handled</li><li>Optional hosts-file blocking for ads and tracker domains</li></ul><p><b>Calm, day or night</b></p><ul><li>A clean Material 3 design in light or dark, with your wallpaper colours or a palette you pick</li><li>On tablets and foldables, the app list and an app's rules sit side by side</li></ul><p><b>Private by design</b></p><ul><li>No root, no account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics</li><li>Filtering happens on your phone through Android's VPN service. Your traffic is not sent to a QuietGuard server</li><li>Update checks stay off unless you turn them on</li></ul><p>QuietGuard is a local firewall, not an anonymity tool: it decides which apps may connect, it does not hide where you are. Because Android allows one VPN at a time, it can't run alongside another VPN app.</p><p>QuietGuard is open source under the GPLv3, built on Marcel Bokhorst's NetGuard, with a new interface in Kotlin and Compose and a packet engine in Rust. Source code, threat model and issue tracker: <a href='https://github.com/bernaferrari/QuietGuard'>github.com/bernaferrari/QuietGuard</a></p><p>Requires Android 8.0 or later.</p>
